Advanced features that could differentiate the app
AI Load Advisor: User enters “2 ACs, fridge, 6 fans, TV and lights,” and the app builds the load profile automatically.
Appliance OCR: Take a photo of an appliance nameplate and extract rated watts/voltage.
Real-Time Monitoring: Connect to a smart meter or energy monitor and compare actual consumption with calculated estimates.
Power Factor Support: Include watts, VA, and power factor for more accurate inverter/generator sizing.
Essential-Load Mode: User selects which appliances should operate during an outage, and the app sizes the backup system around those loads.
Scenario Presets: Normal Home, Load Shedding, Solar Backup, Generator Backup, Full Home Backup.
Multi-Currency/Tariff Support: Useful if the app will serve multiple countries.
Historical Dashboard: Track estimated vs. actual consumption and bills over time.
